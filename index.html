Who We Are

A Labor of
Love & Layers

YasTiramisu began as a single obsession: to craft the most honest, beautiful tiramisu outside of Italy. Every recipe we serve is the result of months of testing, tasting, and refining.

We source our Savoiardi from a small family bakery in Venice, our espresso from a single-origin roaster in Turin, and our mascarpone from a farm in Lombardy. No shortcuts. Only passion.

How It's Made

The Craft

01
The Espresso

We pull fresh shots daily from our Turin single-origin blend. The soak time is timed to the second — enough depth, never bitter.

02
The Cream

Lombard mascarpone, free-range egg yolks, and cane sugar whipped to a ribbon stage. Light as cloud, rich as velvet.

03
The Layers

Each savoiardo is dipped by hand. Each layer is piped with care. No machines, no rushing. Only rhythm and intention.

04
The Rest

24 hours minimum in the cold. The flavors marry slowly. We never serve a tiramisu that hasn't had its time to dream.
